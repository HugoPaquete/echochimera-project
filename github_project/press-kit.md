# Press kit

## One sentence

ECHOCHIMERA is a real-time MIDI instrument in which an AI listens,
decides whether to answer, and performs bounded musical characters — or
silence.

## One paragraph

ECHOCHIMERA is a performance instrument for human–AI improvisation. It
listens to a musician, analyses pitch and gesture, and decides whether to
respond. When it answers, it chooses one of seven characters and performs
a constrained phrase through MIDI; when it refuses, the refusal is part of
the music. The interface makes the system's decisions visible through a
QuadPad, a decision history, a phrase view, a rhythm grid and a signal
trace.

## Five bullets

- Constrained agency: the system can answer, wait or refuse.
- Seven characters, including silence.
- A phenomenological corpus and a Markov model under a response contract.
- An explicit MIDI/CC contract with note safety and panic.
- A visual performance surface designed for observation.
