# Concept

## The question

What happens when an AI performer does not answer immediately?

Most generative systems are designed to produce. ECHOCHIMERA is designed
to listen, decide and sometimes refuse. Its musical identity comes from
constraints, not from unlimited output.

## Listening as composition

The system analyses pitch, energy, onset and timbre before generating
anything. Listening is the first compositional act. The input is treated
as evidence, not as a trigger.

## Constrained agency

The system can answer, wait or return silence. Its decisions are bounded
by:

- an energy/onset gate;
- a response probability;
- one of seven characters;
- a response contract;
- an allow-listed MIDI stream;
- a note ledger that closes what it opens.

The instrument can say “no”. Refusal is part of its voice.

## Heautoscopy

The title uses *heautoscopy* as a metaphor for self-observation: the
performer sees their own listening reflected in the system's decisions.
The interface makes that reflection visible through decision history,
phrase view, signal trace, rhythm grid and Markov graph.

The reflection is not a copy. The system answers with its own material,
timing and refusals.

## Silence

`SILENCE` is one of the seven characters. It is not a muted error or an
empty buffer. The instrument may decide that the correct answer is to
leave the space open.

## Performance as negotiation

The performer negotiates with a system that can answer too much, too
little, too late or too early. Failure and recovery are part of the form:
a stuck note, a lost MIDI port, a panic, a silence that lasts too long.

## Contribution

1. A constraint-first architecture for human–AI performance.
2. A theory-grounded mapping from character to duration, density,
   harmony and silence.
3. A transparent MIDI/CC contract with note safety.
4. A public documentation practice that separates concept from source.

## What this page does not contain

No source code, algorithms, implementation formulae, executable or
configuration. The public material describes the musical and conceptual
system; the implementation remains private.
