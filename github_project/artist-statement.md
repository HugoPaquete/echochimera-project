# Artist statement

ECHOCHIMERA is a performance instrument built around a simple question:
what happens when an AI does not answer immediately?

The system listens to a musician, extracts pitch and gesture, and decides
whether to respond. When it answers, it chooses one of seven characters —
from a held tone to a dense cluster, from an echo to silence — and performs
a bounded phrase through MIDI. When it refuses, the refusal is audible.

I think of the instrument as a heautoscopic surface: the performer sees
their own listening reflected in the system's decisions. The interface
shows the decision history, the last phrase, the pitch trace and the
Markov graph, not to explain the music but to make the system's agency
legible. The audience is invited to watch a negotiation rather than a
demonstration.

The musical material comes from a corpus of phenomenological operators:
sparse long tones, wide leaps, chromatic bands, partial clouds, mass
textures, digital glitches and structured silence. These are not patches.
They are behaviours, and the system can inhabit them only within limits:
a response contract, an allow-listed MIDI stream, a note ledger that
closes what it opens.

The work is not about replacing the performer. It is about composing the
conditions under which a machine can listen, refuse, answer and stop.
Silence is a form of agency. Failure is part of the score. Recovery is a
performance skill.

ECHOCHIMERA is a project by Hugo Paquete, developed in the context of
INET-md / University of Aveiro and Absonus Lab, with support from the
CEEC Individual Scientific Employment Grant and the AI as Catalyst
research programme.
